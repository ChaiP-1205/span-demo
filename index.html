Why these signals
GitHub activity
maps to FITbasic · at signup
Is this the kind of engineering org the product is built for? Public and free, so it's available the instant someone signs up.
Ingestion volume
maps to BUYING WINDOWdeep · streams in over trial
How close are they to a forced decision point on their limits? Doesn't exist until they've actually started sending data.
SRE / DevOps hiring
maps to INTENTdeep · gated lookup
Are they actively investing in the function that owns this call? An external lookup, only worth paying for once basic fit clears a floor.
Together these cover the three questions that qualify an account: fit, buying window, and intent — but only fit is knowable at the moment of signup. The other two are why Span re-scores instead of deciding once and stopping.

Business rules
One account per company
A repeat signup, matched by domain, updates the existing account instead of creating a duplicate lead. Keyed by workspace, not per person.
Idempotent scoring
Re-scoring the same signup does not re-fire a Slack alert or CRM write. Same input in, same result out, no duplicates.
Cost-gated enrichment
Live GitHub lookups run once per new signal. The SRE/hiring lookup only fires for leads that clear a basic-fit floor, not every signup.
Partial-data fit check
A missing signal still yields a score from the other 3 of 4 inputs. Enrichment gaps never block the pipeline.
Adjustable thresholds
Score cutoffs (70 / 40) are settings, so sales and marketing move the line as they learn what converts.
Event-driven re-scoring
Ingestion and usage data trigger a re-score on threshold, not on a fixed schedule — 80% of trial cap or day 20/30, whichever comes first.
